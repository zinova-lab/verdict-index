Review status notice

Posted 22 September 2026

The 69 evaluations on this index were completed between March and May 2026 (37 in March, 24 in April, 8 in May) and published together on 28 August 2026. No routine review has been carried out since publication, so every evaluation is now past the 90-day review date written into its record at the time of evaluation.

VERDICT is moving its review method from a fixed 90-day cycle to trigger-driven reviews — a public CVE, a GitHub security advisory, a CISA KEV entry, a supply-chain compromise, or a change of ownership — with one routine check per year. A full re-verification of the existing 69 evaluations is scheduled in this order: the 24 platforms with a CVE recorded in the trailing twelve months, then the 9 remaining platforms in the agent layer, then the other 36. No record is changed until its sources have been re-checked. Trigger-based reviews apply to every platform throughout.
